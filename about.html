WHO WE ARE

More than a manufacturer. A partner for food businesses.

Food is changing. Consumers are looking for convenience, but they are also becoming more conscious about ingredients, nutrition and the choices they make every day.

At OMG Laxmi Foods, we see this changing food landscape as an opportunity to create products that balance convenience with evolving consumer expectations.

Our core expertise lies in noodles manufacturing, while our approach goes beyond simply producing a finished product. We work across manufacturing, product development, private labelling and food innovation to help businesses create products that are practical, scalable and relevant to today's market.

Alongside our B2B capabilities, our consumer brand Eiyaa allows us to explore our own product ideas and bring new noodle concepts directly to consumers.
