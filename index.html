OUR CONSUMER BRAND

Meet Eiya.

Everyday noodles, thoughtfully reimagined.

Eiya is the consumer-facing noodle brand from OMG Laxmi Foods, created around a simple idea — to make everyday noodles convenient, enjoyable and increasingly aligned with modern food preferences.

While noodles have always been loved for their simplicity and versatility, Eiya gives us a platform to explore what comes next. Our product development journey looks at traditional grains, millet-based recipes, multigrain combinations and protein-enriched concepts to create new possibilities for everyday meals.

From familiar favourites to innovative grain-led formats, Eiya represents our consumer-facing approach to food — combining convenience, flavour and continuous product exploration.
